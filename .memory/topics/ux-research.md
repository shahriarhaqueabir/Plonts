# UX Research — First-Time Gardener (2026-11-19)

Full brief: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-ux-human-centric-design-brief.md

## Key findings
- Beginners are overwhelmed; need "just tell me what to do" step-by-step guidance.
- Top pain points: information overload, low confidence, timeliness, curated/personalized info, minimal planning, remembering tasks, in-garden access, data loss, too much detail, account pressure.
- Design principles: one plant to start, "what can I plant today?", ease-of-growing rating, Legos-style steps, weather-aware reminders, progressive disclosure, plain language, emotional support, in-garden ready, no data loss, quick notes+photos, companion friends/enemies.
- LovelyGardening already does well: local-first, onboarding, catalog, layout canvas, toast, image cache, skip confirm, multi-select.
- Top P0 quick wins: "Start here" empty state, ease-of-growing badge, "Plant now" tag, onboarding progress bar, checkboxes not radios, no login wall.
